# SOP Handling Damaged & Expired Goods
**Makmur Jaya Store | Effective: 2026**

## Objective
To handle damaged or expired goods with clear procedures to minimize losses, ensure product safety, and document every transaction of goods disposal.

## Categories of Goods Handled
1. **Damaged Goods:** Damaged packaging, spilled contents, defective products upon receipt or during storage
2. **Expired Goods:** Food/drinks past the expiration date, medicine/vitamins past the validity period
3. **Slow-Moving Goods:** Slow-moving products that have not sold in 6+ months (Owner's decision)
4. **Customer Return Goods:** Goods returned by customers (damaged, defective, wrong order)

## Procedure for Handling Damaged Goods

### 1. Identification of Damaged Goods
**Upon Receiving Goods (from Supplier):**
- Warehouse staff check every package upon receipt
- If packaging is damaged/contents spilled: DO NOT accept fully, separate to the rejection area
- Record details of damaged goods in the Goods Rejection Form (date, supplier, product name, quantity, cause of damage)
- Photograph damaged goods for documentation

**During Stock Opname or Routine Checks:**
- Sales staff/Warehouse staff conduct routine patrols (1x per day)
- If damaged goods are found on the shelf/in the warehouse: immediately remove & separate
- Record in the Daily Damaged Goods Finding Form

**When Customer Complains:**
- Cashier accepts complaints of damaged goods from customers
- Verify the goods (open packaging if necessary)
- If proven damaged by the store: accept return (recharge payment/exchange goods)
- Record the complained damaged goods in the Customer Return Form

### 2. Separation & Storage of Damaged Goods
- All damaged goods are collected in **Zone 5 (Damaged Goods Holding Area)** in the warehouse
- Clearly label: "DAMAGED GOODS – DATE [date] – FURTHER ACTION REQUIRED"
- Do not mix with normal goods (risk of confusion during restock)
- Record product name, quantity, condition of damage, date found

### 3. Evaluation & Handling Decision
**Damaged Goods from Supplier (Rejection & Return):**
1. Owner reviews damaged goods + Rejection Form
2. If supplier is responsible (damaged during shipping): prepare for return
3. Contact supplier with rejection document + photos
4. Supplier takes damaged goods OR sends replacement goods
5. Record return in the Goods Return Log & Loss Report

**Damaged Goods During Storage (Internal Damage):**
1. Investigate cause: (a) Unstable stacking, (b) Warehouse humidity, (c) Exposure to heat, (d) Defective product damage
2. If due to internal factors (placement error): record as store loss
3. If due to external factors (product defective from factory): return to supplier with documentation

**Final Decision:**
- **If repairable/discount sale possible:** Input into POS system as discounted item, record new price
- **If not sellable:** Delete from system (write-off), record in the Goods Disposal Log
- **If claimable from supplier:** Prepare for return, complete documentation

### 4. Disposal of Unsellable Damaged Goods
- Goods agreed not to be sold: fully separate from POS system inventory
- Input disposal in the "Write-Off Goods" or "Inventory Adjustment" menu (negative value)
- Record disposal reason (damaged, unsellable, expired, policy)
- Request Owner approval before final disposal
- Dispose of goods in the trash bin (if non-hazardous) or special waste area (if chemical substances are present)

## Procedure for Handling Expired Goods

### 1. Identification of Expired Goods
**Routine Checks:**
- Sales staff daily: check expiration dates of goods on the sales shelf (minimum 2x per day: morning when opening, afternoon when restocking)
- Warehouse staff: check expiration dates of goods in the warehouse 1x per week (Friday)
- Automatic filter system (if POS has this feature): alert goods expiring within 7 days

**Status Categories Based on Remaining Shelf Life:**
- **Green (Safe):** Remaining shelf life > 30 days (normal sales display)
- **Yellow (Warning):** Remaining shelf life 7–30 days (discount/move priority, not front display)
- **Red (Alert):** Remaining shelf life < 7 days (remove from shelf, ready for disposal)
- **Black (Expired):** Past expiration date (remove immediately, do not sell)

### 2. Handling Expired Goods (Yellow & Red)
**Yellow Status Goods (7–30 days remaining):**
- Take goods from the main display rack
- Move to the "20–30% Discount Goods" area (if a specific area exists) or the discount rack
- Update price in POS (discount according to policy: 20–30% of the normal price)
- Apply a red sticker label: "EXPIRY DISCOUNT – [X] days remaining – Take today!"
- Record in the Discount Goods Log (date, product, normal price, discount price, qty)

**Red Status Goods (< 7 days remaining):**
- Take directly from the rack & warehouse
- Separate to the "Almost Expired – Do Not Sell" area
- Apply a red label: "DO NOT SELL – EXPIRED [DATE]"
- Prepare for disposal (not sold, discard)

**Black Status Goods (Expired):**
- Take directly, do not sell in any condition
- Separate to the expired goods holding zone
- Apply a black label: "EXPIRED [DATE] – DISCARD"
- Record in the Expired Goods Log (date, product, expiry date, qty, reason)

### 3. Expired Goods Disposal Process
**Disposal Documentation:**
1. Cashier or Owner inputs expired goods into the POS system (Adjustment Inventory, negative value)
2. Record the reason: "Expired on [expiry date]"
3. Verify the quantity removed matches the physical goods
4. Print the disposal report (Inventory Write-Off Report)
5. Request approval from the Owner & sign the document

**Goods Disposal:**
- Expired food items: discard in the trash (do not donate)
- Expired medicine/vitamins: discard in the special medicine waste (if available) or regular covered trash
- Expired cleaning product items: discard in the special waste bin or covered trash
- **Attention:** Do not sell or donate expired goods (health and legal risks)

### 4. Expired Goods Loss Reporting
**Expired Goods Log (Monthly):**
- Identification date
- Product name & SKU
- Original expiry date
- Quantity expired
- Loss value (qty × cost price)
- Reason (not sold, short shelf life, ineffective discount, etc.)

**Analysis & Corrective Actions (Owner):**
- Review at the end of every month: which products expire frequently?
- Evaluate the cause: excessive purchasing? Too high price? Product not popular with the market?
- Action: reduce purchase of that product, increase discount more aggressively, or stop buying from the supplier
- Record expiry trends for more accurate purchasing planning

## Customer Return Handling Procedure

### 1. Receiving Customer Returns
**Return Acceptance Conditions (according to Makmur Jaya Store Return Policy):**
- Items can be exchanged up to 3 days with a receipt
- Exception: food & beverages CANNOT be exchanged/returned
- Items must be in good condition (neat packaging, no signs of opening/use)

**Verification Process:**
1. Cashier receives the return item from the customer
2. Check the purchase receipt (is it real, matching the store, date < 3 days)
3. Check the item (packaging condition, is it still normal, is there a price sticker)
4. If conditions are met: accept the return & record in the Customer Return Form
5. If conditions are not met: explain the reason & refuse (record in the Return Refusal Log)

### 2. Handling Damaged/Defective Returned Items
**If Return is Proven Due to Product Defect from the Store:**
- Cashier verifies the item is not damaged due to customer error
- Refund 100% or offer an exchange for another item of equal value
- Record as Defective Return in the System

**If Return is Due to Customer Error (damaged at home):**
- Politely refuse ("Sorry, the product was not damaged when we sold it")
- If the customer insists: offer a replacement discount (50% of the new price) as goodwill
- Record as Refused Return

### 3. Placing Returned Items into Inventory
- Returned items received: re-verify their quality
- If the condition is good: put them back on the normal sales rack (update stock in POS)
- If there is slight damage (packaging damaged but contents okay): 10–20% discount & place on the discount rack
- Record in the Return Acceptance Log (date, product, qty, final status)

## Checklist for Handling Damaged & Expired Items
- [ ] Damaged items are identified & separated from normal stock
- [ ] Expired items are checked 1x per day (sales rack & warehouse)
- [ ] Yellow status items are moved to the discount area (sticker label)
- [ ] Red & Black status items are segregated (do not sell)
- [ ] Item deletion is input into the system & approved by Owner
- [ ] Items are disposed of in the appropriate trash bin
- [ ] Deletion log is filled out & archived
- [ ] Customer returns are verified according to policy
- [ ] Returned items received are checked & inventoried

## Inventory Loss Standards & Improvement Targets
- **Expired Item Loss Target:** Max 1% of total monthly sales value
- **Customer Return Target:** Max 2% of total transactions
- **Supplier Rejection Target:** Max 3% of receiving volume
- If targets are exceeded: review & create an improvement action plan

## Important Notes
- **Never sell expired or severely damaged goods** (health risk, reputation, legal)
- Every deletion must be audited (complete documentation & Owner approval)
- Identify expired items at least 2x per day to minimize losses
- Analyze expiration trends every month for purchasing optimization
