# SOP for Daily Cash Handling & Deposits
**Makmur Jaya Store | Effective: 2026**

## Objective
To ensure the management of cash, digital payments, and the daily deposit process runs transparently and securely according to accounting standards.

## Execution Time
Every day during store closing (20.45–21.00) and bank deposits according to schedule (Monday & Thursday at 16.00–17.00)

## Responsible Personnel
- Cashier (handling transactions & closing the cash register)
- Owner/Manager (verification, signature, bank deposit)
- Warehouse Staff (security accompaniment during trips to the bank if required)

## Daily Cash Handling Procedures

### 1. Recording Transactions Throughout the Day
- Every sales transaction is automatically recorded in the POS system
- The Cashier records daily operational expenses in the Daily Expense Form:
  - Purchase of tissues/plastic: with a receipt
  - Fuel/employee transportation expenses: Rp 50,000–100,000 per person per day
  - Additional credit/electricity expenses: recorded in real-time
  - Others: with supporting evidence
- All expense receipts are collected and attached to the Daily Expense Book

### 2. Daily Closing Cash Count (At 20.55)
**Cash Counting Process:**
- The Cashier removes all cash from the cash drawer to the table (in front of at least 1 other witness)
- Count in the following manner:
  - Rp 100,000 per stack
  - Rp 50,000 per stack
  - Rp 20,000 per stack
  - Rp 10,000 per stack
  - The remainder of the change is counted per Rp 1,000
- Record the total cash amount in the Daily Cash Closing Form
- Calculate the difference between the expected cash vs. physical cash (cash short or cash over)
- If the difference is > Rp 50,000, the cashier must explain the cause to the Owner/Manager

### 3. QRIS & Digital Payment Verification
- Open the merchant QRIS application (include Makmur Jaya Store QRIS provider details in the system)
- Print the QRIS transaction report for the day (from 07.00–21.00)
- Match the total QRIS transactions with the physical QRIS machine receipt
- Record the total QRIS amount in the Closing Form (e.g., Rp 1,250,000 for that day)
- Perform the same for debit card payments (if a separate card reader machine is available)

### 4. Reconciliation & Deposit Preparation
**Total Sales Calculation:**
- Total Sales = Cash + Total QRIS + Total Debit Card
- Example:
  - Cash: Rp 2,500,000
  - QRIS: Rp 1,250,000
  - Debit Card: Rp 750,000
  - **Total Sales: Rp 4,500,000**

**Net Cash Calculation for Deposit:**
- Net Cash = Total Sales – Operational Expenses – Cash Float
- Example:
  - Total Sales: Rp 4,500,000
  - Expenses: Rp 200,000 (tissues, plastic, etc.)
  - Cash Float for Tomorrow: Rp 200,000
  - **Net Cash for Deposit: Rp 4,100,000**
  - This net cash will be deposited on Monday & Thursday

### 5. Temporary Cash Storage
- Separate the cash float of Rp 200,000 (for the next shift) in a sealed envelope, store it at the Owner's desk
- The remaining cash (net sales) is stored in the safe/vault in the back office
- The safe is opened with 2 keys (1 Owner, 1 Senior Cashier) for maximum security
- Record the safe storage time in the Cash Security Log

### 6. Bank Deposit Process

**Deposit Schedule:** Monday & Thursday at 16.00–17.00 (or flexible according to bank opening hours)

**Deposit Preparation:**
- Count the total amount to be deposited from the safe (sales since the last deposit)
- Prepare the cash in a sealed envelope, record the amount on the envelope
- Prepare the deposit slip from the bank or use the Internal Deposit Form template
- Verify the list of cash to be deposited: amount, quantity, date

**Physical Deposit Process to the Bank:**
- The Owner or Senior Cashier travels to the bank with an accompanying staff member
- Do not go alone for security reasons
- Hand over the cash + envelope to the bank teller
- Receive the deposit slip (bank slip or confirmation slip)
- Verify the amount on the deposit slip must match the amount handed over
- Record the deposit reference number in the Daily Cash Deposit Log

### 7. Documentation & Archiving
- Fill out the Daily Cash Closing Form every day
- Fill out the Cash Deposit Log (each deposit recorded: date, amount, teller/bank, evidence)
- Archive all expense receipts (receipts, QRIS slips) per day in labeled envelopes
- Submit all documents to the Owner/Manager for monthly recapitulation

## Daily Expenditure Standards
- Tissues/Soap: Rp 50,000 (3x per week)
- Shopping plastic: Rp 30,000 (as needed)
- Additional electricity/water: Rp 20,000 (if required)
- Transport/Gas supplier check: Rp 100,000 (flexible)
- Cash register maintenance: Rp 0 (per need, with proof)
- **Average Daily Expenditure: Rp 100,000–150,000**

## Procedure If Cash Short Occurs
1. The cashier immediately reports to the Owner/Manager
2. The Owner & Cashier review the POS transactions for that day
3. Check the receipt for any incorrect entries or customers who did not pay
4. The cashier will bear the short burden if personal error is proven (e.g., cashier forgot, miscounted)
5. If the short is > 2x in 1 month, the cashier will receive a written warning
6. If the short is < Rp 50,000, it can be offset from the employee's bonus for that month

## Daily Cash Handling Checklist
- [ ] All transactions are recorded in the POS
- [ ] Daily expenditures are recorded with proof
- [ ] Cash is counted by 2 people
- [ ] QRIS & debit cards are verified
- [ ] Total sales are calculated correctly
- [ ] Net cash is separated from the cash float
- [ ] Money is deposited into the safe (2 keys)
- [ ] Cash Deposit Log is filled out
- [ ] Bank deposit slips are kept
- [ ] Documents are neatly archived

## Important Notes
- Every transaction must be recorded in the POS; there should be no "off-the-books" sales (unregistered)
- A cash float of Rp 200,000 must be available every day for the next shift's operations
- Bank deposits must be on time (Monday & Thursday), do not delay
- If there is any doubt about the amount of money, recount 3 times before finalizing the entry
