## Products & Services

### Main Product Categories

#### 1. Food & Beverages (45% of total sales)
- **Staple Foods:** Rice, sugar, salt, cooking oil, eggs
- **Canned Foods:** Canned fish, canned meat, canned vegetables, canned fruit
- **Instant Noodles & Snacks:** Various instant noodle brands, chips, candies, chocolate
- **Beverages:** Drinking water, juice, energy drinks, UHT milk, instant coffee, tea

#### 2. Tools & Household Items (25% of total sales)
- **Kitchen Tools:** Knives, rulers, peelers, cooking utensils
- **Cleaning & Hygiene Supplies:** Brooms, dusters, rags, ropes, glasses, plates, bowls, spoons
- **Home Accessories:** Mirrors, combs, rulers, etc.

#### 3. Cleaning & Sanitation Products (15% of total sales)
- **Soap & Shampoo:** Laundry soap, body soap, shampoo, conditioner
- **Home Cleaners:** Floor cleaner, disinfectant, glass cleaning solutions
- **Consumables:** Tissues, diapers, wet wipes

#### 4. Tobacco & Cigarettes (15% of total sales)
- **Cigarettes:** Gudang Garam, Sampoerna, A Mild, Marlboro, local cigarettes
- **Tobacco:** Rolled tobacco, cigarettes, etc.
- **Smoking Accessories:** Lighters, ashtrays
