# Store Closing SOP
**Makmur Jaya Store | Effective: 2026**

## Objective
To close store operations with standardized security procedures, cash register shutdown, and preparation for the next day.

## Execution Time
20:45–21:00 (15 minutes before the official closing time at 21:00)

## Responsible Personnel
- Owner/Manager (Budi Santoso) or Evening Shift Cashier
- Sales Associates for area cleaning
- Warehouse Staff (for security of the back area and doors)

## Standard Procedures

### 1. Stopping Customer Arrivals (20:45)
- Announce to all remaining customers in the store: "Sorry, the store will close in 15 minutes. Please finish your shopping immediately."
- Position the Cashier at the entrance to secure and prevent new customers from entering after 20:50
- Serve customers still in the checkout process until completion

### 2. Sales Area Check (20:50–20:55)
- Sales Associates conduct patrols of all areas to ensure no customers are left behind
- Check restrooms, back display areas, and corners to ensure safety
- Each associate is responsible for one area (1 area per person, 3 people)
- Report findings to the Cashier/Owner if there are any anomalies

### 3. Cash Register System Shutdown (20:55)
- Cashier completes all final transactions
- Print the daily sales report from the POS system (Daily Report)
- Verify the total sales in the system (must match physical cash in the drawer and QRIS log)
- Count the cash in the cash drawer with 2 witnesses (minimum Cashier + Owner/Manager)
- Record the amount of cash in the Daily Cash Closing Form

### 4. Cash and Digital Payment Management
- Separate outgoing cash (operational expenses) from sales
- Record the day's expenses (fuel, mobile credit, etc.) with receipts
- Verify QRIS and debit card transactions in the application (match with machine receipt)
- Place sales cash into the safe (stored in the back office with a dual key)
- Leave a cash float of IDR 200,000 for the next shift if the next day is open

### 5. Cleaning & Sales Area Reset (20:55–21:00)
- Associates clean the floor with broom/mop
- Tidy up products on messy shelves
- Dispose of trash in the outdoor bins
- Check all areas for spills or damage
- Ensure the cashier area is clean and tidy

### 6. Security Check & Facility Shutdown (21:00)
- Deactivate the room's AC
- Turn off all sales lights (ensure only exterior security lights remain on if any)
- Turn off background music
- Shut down the POS system (proper shutdown, wait until it is completely off)
- Verify all back doors (warehouse, restrooms, manager's office) are locked
- Secure all merchandise by checking for fallen or open items
- Turn on the security alarm (if an alarm system exists)
- Ensure exterior lights/signage remain on (for visual security)

### 7. Door and Exterior Area Closing
- Exit all personnel from the store
- Lock all doors (main, back, windows)
- Install locks on the main door and back door
- Verify from the outside that all doors are properly locked
- Check the parking area and surrounding area for any suspicious items/activities

### 8. Closing Documentation
- Fill out the Daily Cash Closing Form (total sales, expenses, remaining cash, 2 signatures)
- Record in the Daily Log Book the date, closing time, total transactions, and important notes
- Hand over the Closing Form to the Owner/Manager for archiving

## Closing Checklist
- [ ] All customers have left
- [ ] Sales area has been patrolled
- [ ] Cashier has finished closing and cash counted by 2 people
- [ ] Cash placed in the safe, IDR 200k cash float prepared
- [ ] Area cleaned and tidy
- [ ] All lights/AC/music turned off
- [ ] POS system shut down
- [ ] Security alarm activated
- [ ] All doors locked and locks installed
- [ ] Closing form filled out

## Important Notes
- Cash register closing time must not exceed 30 minutes (maximum target 21:30)
- If there is a cash discrepancy (cash short/over), record the amount and reason on the Closing Form
- In case of emergency (damage, intruder), contact the Owner/Manager and the police
