# Price & Cashier Error Complaint Guide
**Makmur Jaya Store | Effective: 2026**

## Definition of Price/Cashier Errors

Calculation errors or incorrect pricing that does not match the product label or the POS system price. Examples: price charged is higher than the label, double charge, item not counted, etc.

---

## Types of Price Errors That Can Be Claimed

### ✓ Price Errors That CAN Be Claimed
1. **Label Price Different from Cashier Price:** Item with a price tag of Rp 50,000 but cashier charges Rp 75,000
2. **Double Charge:** Item is charged twice even though only one was purchased
3. **Item Not Counted:** Items purchased are not included in the cashier transaction
4. **Discount Not Applied:** Items should have received a discount but it was not deducted
5. **Rounding Overcharge:** Cashier rounds up when it should round down
6. **Promo Price Not Applied:** Promotional items should be cheaper but were charged the normal price

### ✗ Price Errors That CANNOT Be Claimed
1. **Customer Forgot to Pick Up Items at the Store:** Items are charged but not picked up (customer error)
2. **Price Incorrect According to Customer Request:** Customer requested a specific price but the system has a different price (request error)
3. **Transaction Already Paid & Picked Up:** If you have already left and it has been a long time, verification is difficult

---

## Price Error Claim Procedure

### Scenario 1: Price Error Known DURING Transaction (At the Cashier)

**Steps:**
1. **Observe While Cashier Calculates**
   - Observe the item being scanned & the price appearing on the cashier receipt
   - If you notice something doesn't match (label Rp 50,000 but appears Rp 75,000), immediately point it out

2. **Tell the Cashier**
   - "Excuse me, the price of this item [item name] should be Rp [label amount], why is it Rp [cashier amount]?"
   - The cashier will stop the transaction and check

3. **Cashier Will Verify**
   - The cashier will look at the item label (take it from the shelf to check the price)
   - The cashier will check the price in the POS system
   - If there is a difference, the cashier will correct the price on the receipt (to the correct price)

4. **Price Corrected**
   - If the label price is lower: the cashier will reduce the charge, you pay the lower price
   - If the cashier's price is too high: you save money, the cashier will record the error for training

5. **Finished**
   - Pick up the item & receive the corrected receipt
   - No formal claim is needed, it's done at the counter

**Processing Time:** 2–5 minutes (before the transaction is finished)
**Proof:** Corrected receipt

---

### Scenario 2: Price Error Known AFTER Transaction (At Home/Store)

**Steps:**
1. **Verify Receipt & Label**
   - Compare the cashier receipt with the item label at home
   - Note the price difference (e.g., receipt Rp 75,000, label Rp 50,000)
   - Calculate the difference (Rp 75,000 – Rp 50,000 = Rp 25,000)

2. **Contact the Store Immediately**
   - Phone: (021) xxxx-xxxx
   - WhatsApp: +62 xxx-xxxx-xxxx
   - Email: (if available)
   - Explain: purchase date, what item, receipt price vs. label price, the difference amount

3. **Cashier Will Verify**
   - The cashier will check the price in the POS system for that item
   - The cashier will check the label on the shelf (to verify the label price)
   - The cashier will check your original receipt (for matching)

4. **If Verification is Accepted (Error Proven)**
   - The cashier will record it in the Price Error Complaint Form
   - The cashier will calculate the refund/difference that needs to be returned
   - The cashier will offer ways to return the difference:
     - **Option 1:** Cash refund (if you come back)
     - **Option 2:** Bank transfer (if initial payment was via QRIS/transfer & difference > Rp 50,000)
     - **Option 3:** Shopping voucher (credit for the next purchase)
     - **Option 4:** No refund (if the difference is very small < Rp 5,000, the cashier may waive it)

5. **If Verification is Rejected (No Error/Too Late)**
   - The cashier will explain the reason for rejection
   - Reasons might include: no receipt, older than 7 days, POS price is the same as the charged price
   - You can negotiate with the owner

**Processing Time:** 1–3 days (pending verification & transfer if needed)
**Proof:** Original receipt, photo of the item label, complaint details via WhatsApp

## Price Error Case Examples

### Case 1: Overcharge (Item More Expensive)
- **Situation:** Bought 2L Cooking Oil, price label Rp 48,000, but cashier charged Rp 62,000
- **Difference:** Rp 14,000
- **Cause:** Cashier input error into POS or old price input
- **Solution:** Refund Rp 14,000 (cash, transfer, or voucher)

### Case 2: Double Charge
- **Situation:** Bought 1 bottle of soap, but charged 2 times (Rp 25,000 × 2 = Rp 50,000)
- **Cause:** Cashier accidentally scanned the product twice
- **Solution:** Refund Rp 25,000 (1 bottle)

### Case 3: Uncounted Items
- **Situation:** Bought 5 items but only 4 items were charged
- **Cause:** Cashier forgot to scan 1 item or skipped a scan
- **Solution:** You must pay for the forgotten item (or the cashier will charge again)

## Price Error Claim Deadlines

| Claim Time | Complaint Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| During transaction (< 5 minutes) | **EASIEST TO ACCEPT** | Cashier corrects immediately, no formal claim |
| Same day, before closing | **EASIEST TO ACCEPT** | Receipt is fresh, can be verified |
| 1–3 days | **ACCEPTABLE** | Pending verification, need receipt & proof |
| 4–7 days | **DIFFICULT TO ACCEPT** | Cashier starts forgetting details, verification is complicated |
| > 7 days | **VERY DIFFICULT/REJECTED** | Old receipt, verification is almost impossible |

**Recommendation:** Price error complaints should best be filed **within 3 days** for easier verification.

---

## Tips for Getting Price Error Complaints Accepted

1. **Verify at Checkout:** Pay attention to the transaction & state directly if something doesn't match (most effective)
2. **Keep Receipt:** The receipt is the main proof → keep it for up to 7 days (in good condition, not lost)
3. **Record Details:** Write down the date, time, item, and the price difference
4. **Take Proof Photos:** Take photos of the receipt & item label (for documentation)
5. **Contact Immediately:** Don't delay, contact the store max 3 days after purchase
6. **Be Polite:** Cashiers prefer to help if customers behave well

---

## If Price Error is Rejected

**Possible Reasons for Rejection:**
1. Receipt is lost (no proof)
2. More than > 7 days (verification is difficult)
3. Item label has been changed (price changed in the store)
4. Error from the customer's side (e.g., miscounted by self)

**Options If Rejected:**
1. Negotiate with the store owner (Budi Santoso)
2. Contact the owner via WhatsApp with details & proof
3. The owner may consider a refund as goodwill

---

## Checklist Before Filing a Price Error Complaint

- [ ] Purchase receipt is available (don't lose it)
- [ ] Item is present (price label is clearly visible)
- [ ] Complaint date ≤ 3 days from purchase
- [ ] Price difference has been calculated correctly
- [ ] Photos of receipt & item label are available (optional but helpful)
- [ ] Complaint details are prepared (date, time, item, difference)
- [ ] Ready to explain the situation calmly to the cashier
